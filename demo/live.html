About Robbie

Robbie Conal is an American guerrilla poster artist known for his gnarled, grotesque portraits of political figures. He began turning his paintings into street posters during the Reagan administration in 1986, building a nationwide network of volunteers who wheatpaste his work on city walls in the dead of night.

For four decades his sardonic posters have confronted censorship, war, social injustice, and the environment.

The Work

The store carries signed street posters, limited edition prints (digital inkjet, silkscreen, and lithograph), original drawings and paintings, and books — including Art Attack: The Midnight Politics of a Guerrilla Artist and Artburn.
