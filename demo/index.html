About the Artist

Forty Years of Guerrilla Art

Robbie Conal began plastering his sardonic portraits of politicians on city walls during the Reagan administration in 1986. Using a network of volunteers, he's wheatpasted thousands of posters on city streets across America.

His work has addressed censorship, the Iraq War, climate change, and the ever-present circus of American politics — always with unflinching wit and unforgettable imagery.
